Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information that may be handled when you use Mwalimu.com.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how Mwalimu.com may handle information when you visit or use the platform. We aim to use information only for operating, maintaining, protecting and improving the service, and for responding to people who contact us.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, information may include details you submit when creating or using an account, information you add to a profile, messages or enquiries you send to us, and materials or other content you choose to upload or submit.

Information generated when you use the site

Like most websites, technical information may be processed to deliver pages and keep the service working. This can include information such as your browser or device type, pages requested, approximate access times and diagnostic or security information. The exact information available can depend on your browser, device and the services used to operate the site.

How information may be used

  • To provide features you request, including account-related features.
  • To display, store or manage content you submit where the relevant feature supports it.
  • To respond to enquiries, feedback, reports and support requests.
  • To maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To understand how the platform is used and improve its features.

Service providers

Mwalimu.com uses third-party infrastructure services, including Supabase for backend services and Cloudflare for website hosting and delivery. Information may be processed by these providers as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy terms and practices also apply to the services they provide.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not offer your personal information for sale. Information may be accessible to service providers supporting the platform, or may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect the platform and its users, respond to lawful requests, or address security and abuse concerns.

Your choices and requests

You should avoid posting personal or confidential information in public areas of the platform. You may contact us to ask about information associated with your use of Mwalimu.com, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information handled through the platform. No website or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including operational, security and record-keeping needs.

Children

Mwalimu.com is intended for teachers and the wider education community. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the concern.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform or its practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email adminmwalimu@gmail.com or use the details on our Contact page.

This page describes the platform’s general approach and should be reviewed and updated if Mwalimu.com adds new data collection, advertising, analytics, payment or account features.
