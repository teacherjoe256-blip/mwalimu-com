Good to know

Academy questions

Who is Mwalimu Academy for?

Practising teachers, student teachers, newly qualified educators, school leaders and other education professionals who want to strengthen their skills.

Are these courses already open for enrolment?

This page is the Academy catalogue preview. These are curated external video resources, not yet Mwalimu-hosted courses. A full course platform with enrolment, lessons, assessments and progress tracking can be added in a later phase.

Will I receive a certificate?

The planned learning experience includes certificates of completion after the required course activities and assessments are completed. Any certificate will be clearly identified as issued by Mwalimu; it should not be presented as an accredited qualification unless formal recognition is obtained.

Will courses cover practical and safety-related skills?

Yes. Topics such as first aid and school safety should be developed or reviewed with appropriately qualified professionals. An online awareness course is not a substitute for practical certified training.
