A WhatsApp community for teachers in Uganda

Teaching can be demanding, but educators do not have to work in isolation. The Mwalimu Teachers Community is a WhatsApp group where teachers and people involved in education can connect, exchange ideas and share practical experiences from the classroom.

Join the Mwalimu Teachers WhatsApp Group

Looking for a teachers' WhatsApp group in Uganda? Join the Mwalimu community and connect with fellow educators.

Join the WhatsApp group ↗Contact Mwalimu

What is the Mwalimu teachers community?

It is an online meeting point for educators to connect around teaching and education. Members can use the group to exchange teaching ideas, discuss classroom experiences and stay connected with other teachers.

Who can join?

The community is intended for teachers and people involved in education who want to connect with fellow educators. Please follow the group rules and be respectful of other members when participating.

How to join the teachers' WhatsApp group

  1. Tap the Join the WhatsApp group button above.
  2. WhatsApp will open the community invitation.
  3. Review the invitation and follow the on-screen steps to join.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a WhatsApp group for teachers in Uganda?

Yes. Mwalimu shares a WhatsApp invitation for teachers and educators who want to connect with fellow teachers.

How do I join the Mwalimu teachers group?

Use the WhatsApp invitation button on this page and follow WhatsApp's joining instructions.

Is Mwalimu only a WhatsApp group?

No. mwalimu.site also brings together teaching resources, education jobs, professional development, education updates and practical AI support for teachers.
